What this is

Freud said a dream is a disguised wish, and wrote down the disguise: condense, displace, picture, smooth. Modern labs say the brain makes dreams strange on purpose so it does not overfit to yesterday. VELOMNA runs that disguise as a machine, on everyone's seeds, every ninety minutes. Nobody can predict tomorrow's world, including the machine that builds it.

Free, forever, on any phone browser. No ads, no streaks, no timers, no loot. Built in the open at zeroorigine.com.
